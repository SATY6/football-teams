SLIET Football Cup 2026

Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology

The SLIET Football Cup is the premier inter-hostel football tournament at Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology. Teams from all hostels compete across group stages, knockout rounds, and a grand final to claim the championship.

The tournament promotes sportsmanship, team spirit, and healthy competition among students across all departments and years.
